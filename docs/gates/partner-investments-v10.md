# Partner investments v10
- Applies additive migration db/migrations/20261126_partner_investments_v10.sql before routing the new application. Existing partner, user, equity, compensation, capital contribution, loan and financial tables remain canonical.
- Explicit user links backfilled only for unambiguous existing same-tenant email matches. No new passwords, roles or session grants. Orphaned legacy partner party is restored only when the original ID and normalized document are unambiguous; no change to the partner identifier.
- New investment is an administrative cost-sharing responsibility (not automatically an accounting liability, share capital contribution or expense). Equal allocation in integer cents; immutable investment/share and payment/line records; reversal appends negative lines. Tenant advisory lock, idempotency, scope FKs and deferred total/balance integrity.
- Linked payable must match the full original amount and branch; amortization uses its confirmed settlement, once only. External evidence is explicitly confirmed and unique, not a cash or bank movement. Linked title cannot also be amortized through external mode. No payment transfer or automatic settlement.
- Monthly plan requires finance, capital and compensation permissions. Uses recorded monthly journal result, conservative minimum of registered and reconciled current bank balances, outstanding payables and declared reserves. Current position is not historical balance, excludes physical cash. Approval requires a closed month, one active branch, sufficient funding, balanced journal entries, explicit approval and unchanged preview. Approval records a snapshot, never a salary or profit distribution payment. Tax, legal distribution and banking execution require the existing financial authorization flow.
- Reports added: investment position, shares, payment allocations and approved plans. CSV and browser print/PDF.
- User list now reads official Neon data; no fabricated offline users or success. User creation and password management still rely on the pre-existing authorized backend.
- QA exercised 90,000 / 3, collective 3,000, personal 20,000, individual balance 9,000 and total 67,000; overpayment, partial allocation, reversal, duplicate request, cross-branch rejection; positive and blocked monthly planning; stale preview and repeat approval. Tests ran on isolated branch with rollback. Automated pure cent/rate tests run in integration workflow.
